For group discussion and supervision purposes, it is useful to rate blending on a scale from 0 (patient is hostile toward clinician) to 10 (patient and clinician appear extremely natural together). The degree of blending can be determined from three complementary perspectives: (1) the clinician's subjective feelings, (2) objective evidence from the patient's behavior (e.g., increased eye contact or smiling), and (3) self-report from the patient.
